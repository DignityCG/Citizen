A police officer wants to search my phone. Can he do that?
Under the Nigeria Police Act 2020 (Section 84) and the Cybercrime Act 2015, a police officer cannot search your personal phone without a court warrant — unless you are under lawful arrest and the search is incidental to that arrest.

You have the right to:
· Refuse access politely but firmly
· Ask for the officer's name, badge number, and reason for stop
· Demand a warrant before any device search

Do not resist physically. Record details and report to IPOB or nearest court if violated.
⚖ Police Act 2020 §84 · Cybercrime Act 2015 §38 · ECOWAS Charter Art.6
